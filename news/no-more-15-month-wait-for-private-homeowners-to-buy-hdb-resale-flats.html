No More 15-Month Wait for Private Homeowners to Buy HDB Resale Flats私宅业主购买HDB转售组屋，15个月等待期正式取消

15 months wait period now scrapped等待期正式取消

The 15-month wait rule is gone. Private homeowners no longer have to sit out more than a year before they can buy an HDB resale flat. The government scrapped this rule with immediate effect, ending a restriction that has been in place for years.

The wait was originally introduced as a cooling measure to stop private property owners from jumping straight into the HDB resale market and pushing up prices. It applied whether you still owned your private home or had just sold it, which meant families looking to downsize were stuck waiting before they could even make an offer on a flat.

If you own a private property and want to buy a resale HDB flat, you can do it now. No countdown, no timing your sale around a rule, no forced rental gap in between. For owners who want to right-size or shift housing type, this is a meaningful practical relief that removes a real obstacle from their plans.

15个月等待期已经取消了。私宅业主不再需要等一年多才能购买HDB转售组屋。政府即时废除这项规定，结束了这个已实施多年的限制措施。

这项等待期当初是作为降温措施推出的，目的是防止私宅业主直接涌入HDB转售市场、推高价格。无论你是仍然持有私宅，还是刚刚出售了私宅，这项规定都适用，这意味着想要缩小居所规模的家庭，甚至连出价都要先等上一段时间。

如果你目前持有私宅，想买一间HDB转售组屋，现在就可以行动了。不需要倒数日子，不需要配合规定来安排出售时间，也不需要被迫在中间租房过渡。对于想要调整居所规模或转换房产类型的业主来说，这是一个实实在在的解脱，去除了他们计划中真正的障碍。

What it means for you对你意味着什么
