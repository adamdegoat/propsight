Property Agents Face Tougher CEA Rules, Regulator Also Eyes DIY Home Listings房产经纪面临更严格的CEA规定，监管机构也盯上自行挂牌卖房

The Council for Estate Agencies is tightening its rules for property agents, with one key change requiring agents to show a minimum number of completed transactions before they can renew their licence. The aim is to filter out agents who are not actively practising and to lift the overall standard of the profession.

The regulator is also looking further ahead. The CEA is studying whether to bring private home owners who sell their own properties without an agent under some form of oversight. If you currently list your home on property portals yourself to save on commission, those DIY listings could eventually come with compliance requirements attached.

For most home owners who hire an agent, stricter licensing rules are broadly a good thing. Fewer low-activity agents in the industry generally means the people representing you have more genuine experience and a stronger track record.

If you are considering a private sale to avoid paying commission, it is worth watching what the CEA decides over the next year or so. The rules governing how you advertise and transact could change, and being caught off guard mid-sale would not be a good position to be in.

房产经纪理事会正在收紧对房产经纪的规定，其中一项关键改变是要求经纪在更新执照前须证明完成了最低数量的交易。目的是筛除不活跃执业的经纪，提升整体行业水平。

监管机构也在放眼更远。CEA正在研究是否将自行出售房产、不通过经纪的屋主纳入某种形式的监管范围。如果你目前为了省佣金而自行在房产平台挂牌，这类做法最终可能需要符合合规要求。

对于大多数聘请经纪的屋主来说，更严格的执照规定整体上是好事。行业里不活跃的经纪减少，通常意味着代表你的人拥有更多实际经验和更强的业绩记录。

如果你正在考虑私下出售以节省佣金，值得密切关注CEA在未来一年左右的决定。关于你如何挂牌和交易的规定可能会改变，在交易进行到一半时被打个措手不及，会是很不利的处境。

What it means for you对你意味着什么
Stricter agent standards mean better consumer protection when you hire one, and if you plan to sell your home yourself without an agent, new rules may soon apply to how you can advertise it.更严格的经纪标准意味着你聘请经纪时有更好的消费者保障；如果你打算自行卖房不用经纪，新规则可能很快就会影响你挂牌的方式。
