55% units sold at launch开盘售出单位比例

Dunearn House, the first condominium in Bukit Timah's newly formed Dunearn estate, sold 55% of its units on launch day. The cheapest unit type was fully taken up, while demand for larger and pricier units was more measured.

That split, entry-level units clearing out while bigger ones move more slowly, is a useful read on buyer mood right now. People are willing to commit, but they are watching their budget carefully. Bukit Timah carries a location premium, and buyers appear to be calculating rather than buying on sentiment.

If you are weighing this development or the broader area, 55% is a solid launch result without being a frenzy. It points to genuine demand rather than speculative buying, which is a healthier sign for the project's long-term value. The fact that not everything sold on day one also means you are not being rushed into a decision, so take the time to do proper due diligence.
