Developers get up to 7 years to complete large collective sale sites, easing pressure发展商完成大型en bloc地段的期限延长至最多7年

7 years new en bloc completion deadlineen bloc新竣工期限

Developers who acquire large sites through collective sales now have up to seven years to finish building and sell all units, extended from the previous five-year limit. Missing that deadline under the old rules meant facing heavy additional stamp duty penalties, which made large, complex sites a riskier bet.

For big collective sale sites, five years was often simply not enough time. Construction is more complicated at scale, and market conditions can turn in ways that slow sales of completed units. The penalties meant some developers avoided bidding on large sites altogether, which reduced competition and made it harder for existing owners to get a deal across the line.

For people living in older estates who have been hoping a collective sale might happen, this change improves the outlook. Developers can now take on bigger and more complicated projects without the same pressure to rush through or walk away if timelines extend.

通过集体出售方式收购大型地段的发展商，现在有最多七年时间完成建设并售出所有单位，比原来的五年限期延长了。在旧规定下，一旦超过期限，就要面临高额额外印花税罚款，使得大型复杂地段的投资风险大增。

对于大型en bloc地段来说，五年往往根本不够用。大规模建设本身就更为复杂，市场状况也可能出现变化，拖慢已落成单位的销售进度。罚款机制让部分发展商对竞投大型地段望而却步，削弱了竞争，也让现有屋主更难促成交易。

对于居住在老旧市镇、期待en bloc能够成真的居民来说，这项改变改善了前景。发展商现在可以承接更大、更复杂的项目，而不必担心时间表一拉长就面临同样大的压力，被迫赶工或放弃项目。
