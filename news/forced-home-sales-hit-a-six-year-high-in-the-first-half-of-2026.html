Forced home sales hit a six-year high in the first half of 20262026年上半年强制售房量攀升至六年高点

6-year high mortgagee sales in H1 20262026年上半年的法拍房数量

Bank-initiated forced sales, where a lender takes over and sells a property after the owner defaults on the mortgage, hit a six-year high in the first half of 2026. The rise reflects the squeeze on owners who bought during the years of very low interest rates and are now servicing debt at much higher ones.

The number is not yet at crisis levels, but a six-year high is a clear signal that financial stress among some homeowners is real and building. Tighter lending conditions also make it harder to refinance out of trouble, which leaves fewer options for anyone already running close to the limit.

If your own repayments are already stretching your budget, run the numbers now at a higher rate scenario rather than waiting to see what happens. For buyers who are in no rush, distressed sales can occasionally surface sellers who are more willing to accept a realistic price rather than holding out.

2026年上半年，银行主导的强制出售，即屋主断供后由贷款机构接管并出售房产，数量创六年来新高。升幅反映了那些在极低利率时期购房的屋主，如今以高得多的利率偿还债务时所承受的压力。

目前数字尚未达到危机水平，但六年来的高点是一个明确信号，说明部分屋主的财务压力是真实存在的，而且还在持续累积。收紧的贷款条件也让再融资脱困变得更加困难，这让那些本已在临界线上苦撑的人选择更加有限。

如果你自己的还款已经让预算捉襟见肘，现在就用较高的利率情景把数字算清楚，而不是坐等看看会发生什么。对于不急于出手的买家来说，强制出售案例偶尔会出现更愿意接受务实价格的卖家，而不是一味死守开价。
