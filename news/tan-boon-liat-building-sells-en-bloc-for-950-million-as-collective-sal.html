Tan Boon Liat Building sells en bloc for $950 million as collective sale momentum buildsTan Boon Liat Building以$950 million en bloc成交，集体出售势头持续升温

$950M Tan Boon Liat en bloc priceTan Boon Liat Building en bloc成交价

Tan Boon Liat Building, a commercial and industrial development near Tiong Bahru, has been sold collectively for $950 million. It is one of the bigger en bloc deals to close in recent times and adds to a growing list of collective sales completing in 2026.

A key factor behind the pickup in successful en bloc transactions is that sellers are becoming more grounded in their price expectations. Many past attempts fell apart because owners held out for numbers that developers simply would not pay. That gap appears to be narrowing, and deals that might have stalled a couple of years ago are now getting across the line.

If your estate has been in discussions about a collective sale but has not managed to reach agreement, the current conditions are more favourable than they have been in several years. Developers are actively looking to replenish their land banks, and sellers who set realistic prices have a genuine shot at closing a deal now.

靠近Tiong Bahru的Tan Boon Liat Building，一栋集商业与工业用途于一体的发展项目，已以$950 million集体出售。这是近期较大宗的en bloc交易之一，也让2026年的集体出售成交名单又添新页。

促成近期en bloc交易增多的一个关键因素，是卖家的价格预期变得更加务实。过去许多次尝试之所以失败，正是因为业主坚持发展商不愿接受的价位。如今这一差距似乎正在缩小，一些几年前可能陷入僵局的交易，现在得以顺利成交。

如果你所在的屋苑一直在讨论集体出售，却迟迟未能达成协议，目前的条件比近几年更为有利。发展商正积极寻找地块以补充土地储备，开价务实的卖家，如今确实有机会促成交易。
