Singapore Condo Prices Had Their Slowest First Half Since 2020新加坡condo价格上半年涨幅为2020年以来最慢

Non-landed private home prices in Singapore grew at their slowest pace in the first half of 2026 compared with any year since the pandemic lows of 2020. Condo prices have not gone into reverse, but the rate of growth has come down sharply from the pace seen during the peak years of 2021 and 2022.

Several things have combined to produce this slowdown: higher borrowing costs over the past few years, cooling measures that added stamp duty obligations for certain buyers, and a meaningful increase in new unit supply coming to market. Developers have also priced new launches more carefully after seeing what happens when projects are pushed too aggressively.

For condo buyers, this is the most comfortable buying environment in years. Prices are not collapsing, but they are also not sprinting away from you the way they were during the peak. That gives you the time to compare projects properly, push back on pricing, and make a considered decision without the pressure that drove some regrettable purchases when the market was running hot.

新加坡非有地私宅价格在2026年上半年的升幅，是自2020年疫情低谷以来同期最慢的。Condo价格并没有逆转，但升幅已从2021年和2022年高峰期的步伐大幅放缓。

多项因素合力造成这次降温：过去数年间较高的借贷成本、ABSD等降温措施为特定买家增加了成本，以及市场上新单位供应量的明显增加。发展商在见识过激进定价策略的后果后，在新盘定价上也更加审慎。

对于condo买家来说，这是多年来最舒适的购买环境。价格没有崩盘，但也没有像高峰期那样疾速攀升。这让你有时间好好比较不同项目，与发展商争取更好的价格，做出经过深思熟虑的决定，而不是在市场火热时被迫仓促出手，事后懊悔。
