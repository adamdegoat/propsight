Tougher Rules for Property Agents: Crackdown on Fake Listings and Commission ClarityTougher Rules for Property Agents: Crackdown on Fake Listings and Commission Clarity

Singapore's Council for Estate Agencies is tightening the rules on property agents, focusing on two areas that have frustrated buyers and sellers for years. Fake or bait listings posted mainly to generate leads will face stricter enforcement. On top of that, agents will be required to be upfront about who is paying their commission and how much it comes to.

Fake listings are a well-known irritant in the market. You spot a flat at what looks like a fair price, call up, and are told it was just taken but the agent happens to have something similar. The new rules aim to cut this out by holding agents directly accountable for the accuracy of what they publish.

The commission transparency requirement tackles a separate but equally common source of confusion. Many buyers and sellers do not fully understand whether the agent they are dealing with is working for the other party, being paid by both sides, or solely representing them. Knowing this upfront changes how you weigh an agent's advice and how you approach negotiations. Both changes are straightforward wins for ordinary buyers and sellers who have long deserved clearer dealing.
