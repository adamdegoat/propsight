One Older Bukit Timah Condo Averaged $512,000 Profit Per Unit, Beating Nearby Rivals武吉知马一个老condo平均每单位盈利$512,000，跑赢附近对手

$512,000 average profit per unit平均每单位盈利

A close look at resale profits across Bukit Timah condos found one older development pulling ahead of the pack by a wide margin. On average, sellers in that building walked away with $512,000 more than they paid, outperforming other condos in the same district and in neighbouring Newton.

What makes this notable is that it is not the newest or most talked-about building in the area. Age and prestige are not the only things that drive resale profits. Floor plate design, facilities, proximity to specific amenities, and the historical depth of the buyer pool for a particular development all matter and can vary significantly from one block to the next.

The lesson here is to do your homework at the building level, not just the district level. Two condos on the same street can produce very different outcomes when it is time to sell. Spending time on transaction histories for specific developments before you commit can be worth hundreds of thousands of dollars when you eventually exit.

If you are buying in an established area like Bukit Timah, do not just pick the nicest-looking showflat or the newest launch. Look at how similar units in that exact development have actually performed over five to ten years of resale history.
