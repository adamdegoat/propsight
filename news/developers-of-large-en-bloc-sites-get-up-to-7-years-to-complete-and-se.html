Developers of Large En Bloc Sites Get Up to 7 Years to Complete and Sell Units大型en bloc地块发展商获最多7年时间完工并售出单位

7 years new deadline for en bloc developersen bloc发展商新期限

Developers who buy large collective sale sites now have up to seven years to finish building and sell all units before facing a stamp duty penalty, extended from the previous five-year deadline. The change is specifically aimed at making large en bloc redevelopments more financially workable.

Big sites are genuinely complicated to deliver. After winning an en bloc tender, developers still need to demolish the existing building, secure planning approval, complete construction, and then sell every unit. For a large development, five years was often too tight, especially if planning took longer than expected or the market turned soft during the sales phase. The two-year extension brings the deadline closer to how long these projects actually take in practice.

For owners in older, large condos that might one day be candidates for a collective sale, this change makes your development a more realistic target for developers. Bidders who previously hesitated because of the tight timeline may now be more willing to run the numbers and table an offer.

It will not turn every stalled en bloc attempt into a success, but it removes one genuine structural obstacle that was making developers shy away from big sites. If your development has been discussing a collective sale, this policy shift is worth factoring into those conversations.

购买大型集体出售地块的发展商，现在有最多七年时间来完工并售出所有单位，超期才须缴印花税罚款，从此前的五年期限延长而来。这项改变专门针对让大型en bloc重建项目在财务上更具可行性。

大型地块的开发本来就很复杂。拿下en bloc招标后，发展商还需要拆除现有建筑、取得规划审批、完成建设，再逐一售出所有单位。对于大型项目来说，五年往往太紧，尤其是规划审批比预期花更长时间，或销售期间市场走软的情况下。两年的延长期，让期限更贴近这些项目实际所需的时间。

对于居住在可能有朝一日进行集体出售的大型老condo的屋主来说，这项改变让你的项目成为发展商更现实的目标。此前因为期限太紧而犹豫的竞标者，现在可能更愿意认真算账、提出报价。

这不会让每一个搁置中的en bloc尝试都变成成功，但它确实消除了一个令发展商对大型地块望而却步的真实结构性障碍。如果你的项目一直在讨论集体出售，这项政策调整值得纳入那些讨论的考量之中。
