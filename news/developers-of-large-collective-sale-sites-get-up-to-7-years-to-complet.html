Developers of Large Collective Sale Sites Get Up to 7 Years to Complete Projects大型集体出售地段发展商最多可获7年时间完成项目

7 years new deadline for large en bloc sites大型en bloc地段的新期限

Developers who acquire large collective sale sites now have up to seven years to complete the redevelopment and sell all units before facing penalty stamp duties. The previous deadline was shorter, and the extension is designed to give developers more breathing room when taking on big, complicated projects.

Large en bloc sites are not simple to redevelop. They typically involve significant construction timelines, phased launches and slow-moving planning approvals. A tight deadline forced developers to price in a risk premium, which made it harder for them to offer prices that satisfied existing owners during collective sale negotiations. Extending the runway changes that calculation.

For residents of older, large developments that have been trying to get a collective sale moving, this is a meaningful shift. Lower financial risk for the buyer of the site should translate into more credible offers and fewer deals collapsing over pricing disagreements. If your estate has been stuck in en bloc discussions for a while, this policy change could give stalled negotiations a new lease of life.

购买大型集体出售地段的发展商，现在最多有七年时间来完成重建并售出所有单位，之后才需面对罚款印花税。之前的期限较短，此次延长旨在让发展商承接大型、复杂项目时有更多喘息空间。

大型en bloc地段的重建并不简单。这类项目通常涉及漫长的建设周期、分阶段推出，以及进展缓慢的规划审批。期限过紧迫使发展商在出价时加入风险溢价，导致他们在集体出售谈判中更难开出让现有屋主满意的价格。延长期限，改变了这道算式。

对于那些一直努力推动集体出售的大型旧发展项目居民来说，这是一个重要的转变。地段买家所承担的财务风险降低，应该能带来更有诚意的出价，也减少因定价分歧而导致交易告吹的情况。如果你所在的小区已经在讨论en bloc好一段时间了，这项政策调整或许能为停滞中的谈判注入新动力。
