Tre Ver condo faces termite infestation; developer UOL steps in to help residentsTre Ver condo遭白蚁侵扰，发展商UOL介入协助居民

Residents at Tre Ver, a condo along the Potong Pasir stretch, have been dealing with a termite infestation that has affected multiple units. Developer UOL stepped in to assist those affected, which is notable because the project is relatively new. Most buyers would not expect a newer development to face this kind of issue.

Termites can do serious structural damage if left unchecked, and they are not exclusively a problem for old buildings. The surrounding environment, construction materials, and how well a development is maintained all play a role. A new address does not automatically mean you are protected.

Before buying any condo, it is worth checking with the management corporation on the pest control schedule and whether there have been any past incidents. Beyond that, look at the developer's track record of standing behind their projects after handover. A developer who shows up when problems surface is genuinely valuable, and this case is a useful reminder to find out who you are dealing with before you sign.

位于Potong Pasir一带的Tre Ver condo，多个单位遭遇白蚁侵扰，发展商UOL已介入协助受影响的居民。值得关注的是，这个项目相对较新，而大多数买家大概不会预料到新楼盘会面临这类问题。

白蚁若不及早处理，可造成严重的结构性损害，而且绝非旧楼的专利。周边环境、建筑材料，以及楼盘的日常维护状况，都是影响因素。地址新不等于自动免疫。

购买任何condo之前，值得向管理委员会询问虫害防治的时间表，以及过去是否有过相关事故记录。此外，也要了解发展商在交房后出现问题时的处理口碑。一个在问题出现时愿意站出来负责的发展商，价值是实实在在的。这个案例也是一个提醒，在签约之前，先弄清楚你打交道的是什么样的发展商。
