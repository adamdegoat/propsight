HDB Resale Prices Fall For Second Straight Quarter, But Million-Dollar Flat Sales Hit Record HighHDB转售价连续第二季下跌，但百万元组屋成交量创历史新高

-0.3% hdb resale price change q2HDB转售价变动（第二季）

HDB resale prices fell 0.3% in the second quarter of 2026, marking the second straight quarterly decline. In the same period, the number of flats sold for $1 million or more hit a record high. Both statements are true at the same time, and that tells you more about the market than either figure does on its own.

What is happening is a split. For most flat types in most estates, prices are softening and buyers have more negotiating room than they did a year ago. But for large, well-located flats in mature estates with good transport or school access, demand is intense and sellers are still finding buyers willing to pay exceptional prices.

If you are a buyer, the headline about falling prices is encouraging in general, but check the actual transaction data for the specific block and street you want before assuming you can bargain hard. If you are a seller, resist the temptation to price based on the million-dollar headlines unless your flat genuinely competes in that bracket. The market is sorting itself clearly by location and quality.

HDB转售价在2026年第二季下跌0.3%，连续第二个季度录得跌幅。同一时期，售价达到$1 million或以上的组屋数量却创下历史新高。两件事同时为真，放在一起看，比任何单一数字都更能说明市场的实际状况。

正在发生的是一种分化。对于大多数屋型、大多数地区的组屋来说，价格正在走软，买家比一年前拥有更多议价空间。但对于那些位于成熟市镇、交通便利或名校附近的大型优质组屋，需求仍然强劲，卖家依然找得到愿意出高价的买家。

如果你是买家，关于价格下跌的头条新闻大体上令人鼓舞，但在出手前，务必查看你心仪的那栋楼和那条街的实际成交数据，再决定能否大力压价。如果你是卖家，除非你的组屋确实在百万元级别中具竞争力，否则不要参照那些百万成交的头条来定价。市场正按地段和品质清晰地分层。
