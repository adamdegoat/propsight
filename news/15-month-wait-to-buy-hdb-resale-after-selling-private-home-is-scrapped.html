15-Month Wait to Buy HDB Resale After Selling Private Home Is Scrapped卖掉私人房产后购买HDB转售组屋须等15个月的规定正式取消

15 months wait-out period scrapped等候期取消

The government has scrapped the 15-month waiting period that used to apply to private home owners who wanted to buy an HDB resale flat after selling their private property. The change took effect immediately.

Before this, if you sold your private home and wanted to downsize into an HDB resale flat, you had to sit idle for more than a year before you could make a move. That created a real planning headache, particularly for families managing care for ageing parents, a return from overseas, or a transition timed around school terms. Five groups are directly affected: those downsizing, those buying for elderly parents, Singaporeans returning from abroad, and a couple of other categories where the wait was especially disruptive.

In practical terms, if you own a private property today and want to move into an HDB resale flat, you can now sell and buy in sequence without a forced gap. That simplifies the whole process considerably.

This matters most for the growing number of households who are weighing whether to cash out of private property and step back into the HDB market. The 15-month rule was a real deterrent. Removing it means the flow between these two markets can happen more smoothly.

政府已取消原来适用于私人房产屋主的15个月等候期，过去，他们卖掉私人房产后，须等满15个月才能购买HDB转售组屋。这项改变即时生效。

此前，如果你卖掉私人住宅想缩小规模搬进HDB转售组屋，必须闲置超过一年才能出手。这对规划造成很大困扰，尤其是需要照顾年老父母、从海外回国，或要配合学校开学时间安排过渡的家庭。五类人群直接受影响：缩减住房规模者、为年迈父母购房者、从海外回国的新加坡人，以及另外两类等候期影响特别大的群体。

实际上，如果你目前持有私人房产、想搬进HDB转售组屋，现在可以先卖后买，中间不必被强制搁置。整个过程大大简化了。

这对越来越多正在考虑套现私人房产、重返HDB市场的家庭来说，意义最为重大。15个月的规定确实是一个实实在在的障碍。取消之后，两个市场之间的流动会顺畅得多。
