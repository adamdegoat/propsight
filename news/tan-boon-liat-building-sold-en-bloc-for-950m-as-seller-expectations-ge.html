Tan Boon Liat Building Sold En Bloc For $950M As Seller Expectations Get More RealisticTan Boon Liat Building以$950M集体出售，卖家预期趋于务实

$950M en bloc sale priceen bloc出售价格

The Tan Boon Liat Building along Outram Road has been sold collectively for $950 million, one of the larger en bloc deals in recent memory. Analysts say the transaction reflects a shift in how sellers in older developments are thinking about pricing, with more owner groups now willing to accept realistic figures rather than waiting indefinitely for a peak valuation.

En bloc activity went quiet for a stretch because sellers were quoting prices that made it impossible for developers to pencil in a profit, especially with construction costs still high. What appears to have changed is that enough owners have accepted that a reserve price needs to make commercial sense for a buyer before any deal can happen.

If you live in an older development, this deal is a signal that the en bloc cycle has found some momentum again. But whether your building can follow depends on whether a strong enough majority of owners can agree on a reserve price that is realistic, not just aspirational. Many buildings that failed earlier rounds did so because a small group of owners held out for a figure no developer would touch.

Outram Road的Tan Boon Liat Building以$950 million集体出售，是近年来规模较大的en bloc交易之一。分析师表示，这宗交易反映出旧楼屋主在定价思维上的转变，越来越多的屋主群体愿意接受实际的价格，而不是无了期地等待估值的高峰。

En bloc活动之前一度沉寂，原因是卖家开价太高，令发展商难以算出合理的利润空间，加上建筑成本持续高企。如今的改变，在于足够多的屋主接受了一个现实：底价必须让买家在商业上说得通，交易才能成事。

如果你住在较旧的楼盘，这宗交易是一个信号，说明en bloc周期已重新找到动力。但你的楼盘能否跟上，关键在于是否有足够多数的屋主能就一个务实的底价达成共识，而不是单凭期望来定价。许多楼盘之前集体出售失败，正是因为少数屋主坚持要一个没有发展商愿意接受的价格。
