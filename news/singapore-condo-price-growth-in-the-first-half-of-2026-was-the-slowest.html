Singapore condo price growth in the first half of 2026 was the slowest since the pandemic year of 2020新加坡2026年上半年condo价格涨幅创2020年疫情年以来同期最低

Condo price growth in the first half of 2026 was the slowest recorded for any first half since the pandemic year of 2020, according to URA figures. The combination of government cooling measures, higher borrowing costs filtering through the market, and buyers choosing to take their time has clearly taken the momentum out of the segment.

This marks a genuine shift from the conditions of the past few years. Between 2021 and 2023, the condo market moved fast enough that buyers regularly felt rushed into decisions and often lost out on units they wanted. That urgency has eased considerably, and the market dynamic feels fundamentally different now compared to that period.

For buyers who felt squeezed out during those years, this is the most buyer-friendly environment the condo market has offered in some time. You have more time to do your homework, sellers are more willing to negotiate, and the pressure to overpay just to secure a unit has largely lifted. Prices are not cheap, but the balance of power between buyers and sellers has shifted in a way that is real and worth acting on if you have been waiting for an opening.

根据URA数据，2026年上半年condo价格增幅，是自2020年疫情年以来同期录得的最低增速。政府降温措施、较高借贷成本逐渐渗透市场，加上买家选择放慢脚步，多重因素叠加，已明显抽走了这一板块的动力。

这与过去几年的市场状况相比，是一次真实的转变。2021年至2023年间，condo市场移动之快，使买家经常感到必须仓促做决定，而且屡屡与心仪的单位失之交臂。如今这种紧迫感已大为缓解，市场的整体氛围与那段时期相比，从根本上已截然不同。

对于在那几年感到被挤出局的买家来说，这是condo市场近年来提供的最有利于买家的环境。你有更多时间做功课，卖家更愿意协商，被迫高价抢房的压力也基本消散。价格并不便宜，但买卖双方之间的力量对比已发生了真实的转变，如果你一直在等待一个入场的机会，现在是认真考虑的时候了。
