Condo Resale Volumes Picked Up in June 2026 as Buyers Returned2026年6月买家回归，condo转售交易量回升

Private condo resale transactions picked up in June 2026 after a quieter stretch earlier in the year. More buyers came back to the secondary market, pushing volumes higher compared to the months before.

The resale condo market had been subdued through parts of 2025 and into early 2026, as buyers adjusted to elevated prices and tighter borrowing conditions. The June uptick suggests some of that hesitation is starting to lift, likely helped by mortgage rates pulling back from their peaks and giving buyers more confidence about what they can afford.

For sellers, June was an encouraging sign that more genuine buyers are out there again. If you have been sitting on the fence about listing your unit, conditions are more favourable now than they were earlier this year. That said, do not price off last year's peak valuations. Buyers who have returned are paying attention to value. Price at current market levels and your chances of completing a sale are considerably better.

私人condo转售交易在2026年6月回升，结束了年初以来一段较为淡静的时期。更多买家重返二手市场，推动交易量较前几个月走高。

condo转售市场在2025年部分时段及2026年初一直处于低迷状态，原因是买家对偏高的价格和更严格的借贷条件持观望态度。6月的回升迹象表明，部分犹豫情绪正在消散，这很可能与房贷利率从高峰回落、给予买家更大的负担信心有关。

对于卖家而言，6月是一个令人鼓舞的信号，说明市场上重新出现了更多真实的买家。如果你一直在观望要不要挂牌，现在的条件比今年初更有利。不过，不要参照去年的高峰估值来定价。回归的买家很注重性价比。以当前市场水平来定价，成交的机会将大得多。
