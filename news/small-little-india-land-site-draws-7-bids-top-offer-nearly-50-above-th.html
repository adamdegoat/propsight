Small Little India Land Site Draws 7 Bids, Top Offer Nearly 50% Above the Second小印度小地块引来7份竞标，最高出价比次高出价高出近50%

~50% gap between top 2 bids头两名出价之间的差距

A government land sale tender in Little India drew seven developer bids, but the competition was not really close. The top offer came in almost 50% above the second-highest bid, a gap that is well outside the normal range for competitive tenders.

In most land tenders, serious bids tend to cluster within 10 to 20% of each other because developers are running similar assumptions on construction costs, expected selling prices, and target returns. A gap of nearly 50% means the winning developer is either seeing something very specific about that location that others are not, or they are willing to accept a much tighter margin just to secure the site.

Either way, buyers who are interested in a new launch on this site should expect pricing that reflects the high land cost. Developers do not overpay for land and then undercut themselves on launch prices. That premium gets passed down the chain.

If you are tracking new launches in the Little India area, use this land sale result as your anchor. Budget accordingly and compare carefully against resale options nearby before committing.
