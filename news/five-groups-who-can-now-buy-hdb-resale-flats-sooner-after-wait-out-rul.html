Five groups who can now buy HDB resale flats sooner after wait-out rule was scrapped等待期取消后，五类人可以立即购买HDB转售组屋

15 months wait-out period removed等待期已取消

The government has scrapped the 15-month wait-out period that previously stopped private property owners from buying an HDB resale flat straight away. Under the old rule, you had to sell your private home first and then wait over a year before you could complete an HDB resale purchase.

Five groups felt that rule most acutely. People going through a divorce who needed to separate their living arrangements quickly. Those who inherited a private property without planning to and suddenly found themselves locked out of the HDB resale market. Older owners who wanted to right-size from a large private home to something smaller and more affordable. In each case, a 15-month delay on top of an already difficult life transition made a hard situation harder.

The rule is now gone and the change is immediate. If you or someone in your family is in any of these situations, there is nothing to wait out. You can move ahead with an HDB resale purchase as soon as you are ready.

政府已经废除了15个月的等待期规定。在旧规定下，私宅屋主必须先卖掉私宅，然后再等超过一年，才能完成HDB转售组屋的购买手续。

有五类人对这条规定感受最为深刻：正在办理离婚、急需分开居住安排的人士；无意中继承了私宅、结果发现自己突然被挡在HDB转售市场门外的人；以及年长屋主，想从大面积私宅搬到更小、更实惠的单位。对这些人来说，在本已艰难的人生转折关头，还要多等15个月，只会让处境更加难熬。

规定已经取消，而且即时生效。如果你或你的家人处于上述任何一种情况，不需要再等了。只要准备好，随时可以着手购买HDB转售组屋。

What it means for you对你意味着什么
