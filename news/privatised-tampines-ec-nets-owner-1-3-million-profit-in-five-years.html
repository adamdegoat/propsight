Privatised Tampines EC Nets Owner $1.3 Million Profit in Five Years淡滨尼EC私有化后屋主五年净赚$1.3 million

$1.3M profit in 5 years五年盈利

A privatised executive condo unit in Tampines recently changed hands and the seller pocketed $1.3 million more than they paid, all within five years of buying. That is a remarkable return by any measure.

ECs are a hybrid housing type. When first launched, only eligible buyers can purchase them, subject to income ceilings and citizenship rules similar to those for HDB. After the minimum occupation period and then full privatisation, the flat can be sold to anyone, including foreigners and permanent residents. That much wider buyer pool drives up demand and, with it, prices.

For buyers who currently qualify under the EC eligibility rules, the long-term math is hard to ignore. You buy in at a price that is often lower than a comparable private condo, and once the development privatises, you are competing for buyers from the entire open market. This particular Tampines unit shows what that can look like in practice.

If you are weighing a new EC launch against a private condo at similar price points, factor in this privatisation effect. The entry rules are stricter, but the exit potential can be meaningfully larger.

EC是一种混合型住房。新盘推出时，买家须符合资格条件，包括收入上限和类似HDB的公民身份要求。过了最低居住期、完成私有化之后，单位可以卖给任何人，包括外国人和永久居民。买家群体大幅扩大，带动需求上升，价格自然也跟着涨。

对于目前符合EC资格的买家来说，长期的数字很难忽视。入手价格通常比同等私人condo低，等到项目私有化后，整个开放市场的买家都会来竞购你的单位。淡滨尼这个案例，正好展示了实际情况会是怎样的。

如果你正在比较新推出的EC和同价位的私人condo，记得把私有化这个效应考虑进去。入场规则虽然较严，但退出时的潜在收益可以大得多。
