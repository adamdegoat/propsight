$1M+ HDB resale flats surveyed受访的HDB转售组屋

A new survey has looked at who is actually paying $1 million or more for HDB resale flats, and the profile is more varied than most people assume. The buyer pool at this price level spans a wider range of ages, household types, and financial situations than the standard image of a high-earning couple trading up.

The survey also points to a shift in how buyers at this level are making decisions. Their priorities and behaviour are changing in ways that are reshaping what sells quickly at the top end of the resale market and what does not.

If you own a large flat in a popular mature estate and are thinking about listing at a premium price, understanding who your real buyer is gives you an edge. Pitching to the right person and addressing what they actually care about is what separates a clean sale at a strong price from a flat that lingers on the market.

一项最新调查分析了谁在真正花$1 million或以上购买HDB转售组屋，结果显示买家群体比大多数人想象的更加多元。在这个价格层次，买家涵盖更广泛的年龄、家庭结构和财务状况，并非人们刻板印象中的高收入夫妻升级换房。

调查同时指出，这个价格层次的买家在做决定时，其优先考量和行为方式正在改变，并且正在重塑转售市场高端交易中，哪些单位卖得快、哪些卖不动。
