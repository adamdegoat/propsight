HDB Million-Dollar Resale Market Set for More Action After Wait-Out Rule Scrapped等待期取消后，HDB百万元转售市场预计将更加活跃

$1M HDB million-dollar flat thresholdHDB百万元组屋门槛

With private homeowners now free to buy a resale HDB flat straight away, analysts expect competition to heat up at the top end of that market, specifically among large flats already trading above S$1 million.

Million-dollar HDB flats have become a regular feature of the resale market, concentrated mainly in mature estates like Bishan, Toa Payoh, Queenstown and the central corridor. These are exactly the flats that cash-rich private downgraders tend to go after. Before this rule change, those buyers had to wait more than a year before acting. That barrier is now gone, and some of them will move quickly.

If you are hunting for a large flat in a popular estate, expect to compete with buyers who have just come from private property and often have strong budgets to match. Get your finances sorted before you start viewing, know your maximum offer, and be ready to move when you find the right unit. Hesitating is now more costly than it was a week ago.

随着私宅业主现在可以直接购买HDB转售组屋，分析师预计顶端市场的竞争将会升温，尤其是已经以超过S$1百万成交的大型单位。

百万元HDB组屋已经成为转售市场的常客，主要集中在成熟市镇，如碧山、大巴窑、女皇镇以及中部走廊一带。这些正是手头宽裕、从私宅降级的买家最感兴趣的单位。在规定改变之前，这些买家须等待一年多才能行动。这道门槛现在已经移除，其中一些人会迅速出手。

如果你正在热门市镇寻找大单位，要准备好与刚从私宅市场出来、往往预算充裕的买家竞争。在开始看房之前先理清财务状况，清楚自己的最高出价，一旦找到合适的单位就要迅速行动。犹豫不决的代价，现在比一星期前高多了。
