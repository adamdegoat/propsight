Survey Reveals Who Is Really Buying Million-Dollar HDB Flats, Including One Surprising Trend调查揭晓：谁在买百万元HDB组屋？背后还有个意外规律

A new survey has put some flesh on the bones of who is actually buying HDB resale flats priced above $1 million. Beyond confirming that demand exists, it profiles these buyers more specifically and flags an unexpected pattern in how some of these purchases are being made.

Million-dollar HDB transactions have moved from novelty to regular occurrence, particularly in mature estates where large flats in central locations regularly cross the seven-figure mark. Knowing who is driving demand at the top end is more useful than just watching the transaction count go up.

For anyone shopping in popular or mature estates where these prices are increasingly common, this kind of buyer profile matters. It tells you who you are actually competing against and what their motivations and financial positions might look like, which is practical intelligence when you are deciding how hard to push on an offer or whether a particular flat is worth the fight.

一项新调查终于让外界对$1百万以上HDB转售组屋的买家有了更具体的了解。调查不只是印证了这类需求的存在，还进一步勾勒出买家的面貌，同时点出了部分交易中一个出乎意料的规律。

百万元HDB交易早已不再新奇，在成熟市镇尤为如此，地段好、面积大的单位，成交价突破七位数已是常见现象。比起单纯看成交数字往上涨，搞清楚是谁在撑起这个价位的需求，才更有参考价值。

如果你正在热门或成熟市镇物色组屋，而这类价位又越来越普遍，买家画像对你来说就很有意义了。它能告诉你，你真正在跟什么样的人竞争、对方可能有什么动机、财力大概如何，当你要决定出价力度，或者某个单位值不值得去争的时候，这些都是实用的参考。
