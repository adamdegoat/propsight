Tre Ver Condo Hit By Termite Infestation, Developer UOL Helping Affected UnitsTre Ver Condo爆发白蚁问题，发展商UOL协助受影响住户

Termites have been found at Tre Ver condominium along Potong Pasir Avenue 1, with multiple units affected. Developer UOL has stepped in to assist residents, though the full scope of damage and the treatment plan are still being worked through.

Termite infestations in concrete buildings are more common than most people expect, especially in developments near mature greenery. The insects can spread through walls and flooring over time, and by the time you spot visible damage, the problem has usually been building for a while.

For anyone buying a resale unit in any condo, this is a clear reminder to ask the seller and the management corporation directly about pest history before you commit. Ask for copies of annual general meeting minutes too, since pest issues and maintenance spending should show up there. If you are specifically looking at Tre Ver, find out which units were affected and confirm what treatment was done and when before you sign anything.

白蚁出现在Potong Pasir Avenue 1的Tre Ver公寓，多个单位受到波及。发展商UOL已介入协助住户，但具体受损范围和处理方案目前仍在评估当中。

白蚁侵袭钢筋混凝土建筑，其实比很多人以为的更普遍，尤其是靠近成熟绿化地带的楼盘。白蚁会随着时间慢慢蔓延至墙壁和地板，等到出现明显损坏时，问题往往已积累了一段时间。

如果你在考虑买任何condo的转售单位，这件事是一个明显的提醒：签约前一定要直接向卖方和管理公司询问防虫历史。也可以要求查阅历年常年大会的会议记录，因为虫害问题和维修开销通常会在那里有所记载。如果你特别在看Tre Ver的单位，更要弄清楚哪些单位受到波及，确认曾做过什么处理、是何时进行的，再决定是否签约。
