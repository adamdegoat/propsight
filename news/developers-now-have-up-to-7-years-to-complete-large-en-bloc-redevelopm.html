Developers Now Have Up to 7 Years to Complete Large En Bloc Redevelopments发展商可获最长7年时间完成大型en bloc重建项目

7 years max redevelopment deadline for large sites大型地段的最长重建期限

The government has extended the deadline for developers to complete projects on large collective sale sites, raising the maximum to seven years from the date they acquire the land. The change eases a key financial pressure that was making big en bloc deals hard to structure.

When a developer buys a collective sale site, they pay Additional Buyer's Stamp Duty upfront, which is refunded if they finish and sell all units within the required timeframe. For large sites with hundreds of units, the original window was often too tight to be realistic given the sheer scale of planning, approvals, construction and marketing. That made it difficult for developers to price a bid confidently, so many large sites simply did not attract serious offers.

For homeowners in older developments that have been exploring a collective sale, this change makes it more viable for a developer to table a credible bid on a big site. Do not expect anything to happen overnight, but some large collective sale proposals that were previously too risky to price may now find serious buyers over the coming years, adding future supply to the new homes pipeline.

政府已延长发展商在大型集体出售地段完成项目的期限，从取得土地之日起最长可达7年。这一改变减轻了让大型en bloc交易难以成形的关键财务压力。

当发展商购买一个集体出售地段时，须预先缴付额外买方印花税（ABSD），若能在规定期限内完成并售出所有单位，则可获退还。对于拥有数百个单位的大型地段而言，原有的期限在规划、审批、施工和营销的庞大工作量面前往往显得过于紧迫。这使得发展商难以自信地给出竞标价，导致许多大型地段根本吸引不到认真的出价。

对于正在探讨集体出售可能性的旧发展项目业主而言，这一改变使发展商更有条件对大型地段提出具竞争力的竞标。不要期待事情一夜之间发生，但一些此前因风险过高而难以定价的大型集体出售项目，在未来数年内或许能够找到认真的买家，为新房市场注入未来供应。
