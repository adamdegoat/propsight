Private property owners can now buy HDB resale flats without waiting 15 months私宅屋主购买HDB转售组屋，无需再等15个月

15 months wait removed for private owners私宅屋主等待期取消

The 15-month wait-out period for private property owners wanting to buy an HDB resale flat has been removed. Under the rule that just got scrapped, you had to dispose of your private home first, then wait more than a year before you could complete an HDB resale purchase.

The government announced the change and it took effect immediately, with no transition period. Anyone who was sitting in limbo counting down the months no longer needs to.

In plain terms, if you own a condo or landed property and want to move into an HDB resale flat, whether to cut costs, right-size, or for any other reason, you can now sell your private home and buy the HDB flat in whatever order and on whatever timeline suits you. The forced gap in the middle is gone.

私宅屋主购买HDB转售组屋所须遵守的15个月等待期已经废除。根据刚被取消的旧规定，你必须先处置私宅，然后等待超过一年，才能完成HDB转售组屋的购买程序。

政府宣布这一改变并即时生效，没有任何过渡期。原本还在倒数等待的人，现在不需要再等了。

简单来说，如果你拥有condo或有地住宅，想搬入HDB转售组屋，不管是为了节省开支、缩小居住规模，还是任何其他原因，你现在可以以任何顺序、按照自己的时间表，卖掉私宅、买入HDB。中间那段被迫等待的空档期已经成为历史。
