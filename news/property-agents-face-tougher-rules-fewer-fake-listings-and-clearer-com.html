Property Agents Face Tougher Rules: Fewer Fake Listings and Clearer Commission Fees房产经纪将面对更严规定：杜绝假房源，佣金须透明

The Council for Estate Agencies is tightening how property agents must operate, targeting two long-standing frustrations in the market: fake or misleading listings, and murky commission structures. The new measures are meant to raise baseline standards across the industry.

Fake listings, where an agent posts a property that is already sold or does not exist just to generate calls, have been a persistent complaint from buyers and renters in Singapore. Commission arrangements have also been confusing for buyers in particular, who sometimes do not know who is paying the agent or how much. These rules push agents to disclose fees upfront and to only advertise genuine, available units.

In practice, you should start seeing fewer dead-end listings that waste your time. If you are selling, ask your agent to put the commission in writing before you grant any exclusive. If you are buying, expect agents to be more straightforward about how they are paid and whether they are co-broking with another agent. Neither change is dramatic, but both make the process a bit more honest.

房地产经纪理事会正在收紧房产经纪的操作规范，针对市场上两个长期存在的问题：虚假或误导性房源，以及不透明的佣金结构。新措施的目的是提升整个行业的基本标准。

假房源，即经纪刊登已售出或根本不存在的单位来吸引来电，一直是新加坡买家和租客的投诉焦点。佣金安排对买家来说也往往不够清晰，有时甚至不知道是谁在付经纪费、又付了多少。这些新规定要求经纪预先披露收费，并只能刊登真实可购的单位。

实际上，你应该会开始看到更少浪费时间的无效房源。如果你在卖房，在给予经纪独家代理权之前，要求他们将佣金写进合约。如果你在买房，经纪在收费方式以及是否与其他经纪联合代理方面，将会更加坦诚。两项改变都不算重大，但都让整个过程更加诚信。

What it means for you对你意味着什么
When you use an agent to buy or sell, you should soon see fewer bait-and-switch listings and have a clearer picture of what your agent will cost you.当你通过经纪买卖房产时，很快就会看到更少的诱饵式房源，也能更清楚地了解经纪的收费情况。
