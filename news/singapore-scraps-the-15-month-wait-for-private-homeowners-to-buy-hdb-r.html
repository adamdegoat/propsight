Singapore Scraps the 15-Month Wait for Private Homeowners to Buy HDB Resale Flats新加坡取消私宅屋主购买HDB转售组屋的15个月等待期

15 months wait-out period now removed等待期已正式取消

The government has scrapped the rule that forced people who sold a private home to wait 15 months before they could buy an HDB resale flat. The change took effect immediately, with no transition period attached.

The 15-month wait was introduced as a cooling measure to stop private home sellers from immediately recycling their sale proceeds into the HDB resale market and pushing flat prices up. Removing it now signals that policymakers are comfortable enough with overall market conditions to lift that particular brake.

If you sold a private property recently and have been watching the clock, you can act straight away. For sellers of HDB resale flats, this opens up a wider pool of motivated buyers who no longer have to time their purchase around an arbitrary deadline. Expect some pent-up demand to surface in the months ahead as former private homeowners who were waiting out the 15 months enter the market at once.

政府已正式废除规定，私宅卖家须等候15个月，才能购买HDB转售组屋。新措施即时生效，没有任何过渡期。

当初推出这15个月等待期，是为了防止私宅卖家把套现资金立即转入HDB转售市场、推高组屋价格。如今取消这项规定，意味着政策制定者认为整体市场状况已趋于稳定，有条件撤除这道「刹车」。

如果你近期刚卖掉私宅，一直在掐着日子等，现在可以马上出手了。对HDB转售组屋的卖家来说，这意味着买家群体扩大了，购房者不必再为了一个时限而刻意调整入市时机。预计接下来几个月，先前一直在等待期内观望的前私宅屋主会陆续入市，积压的需求将集中释放。

What it means for you对你意味着什么
