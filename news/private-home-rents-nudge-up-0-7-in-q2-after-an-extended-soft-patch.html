Private Home Rents Nudge Up 0.7% in Q2 After an Extended Soft Patch私宅租金第二季小升0.7%，结束持续一段时间的疲软

0.7% private rent increase q2私宅租金涨幅（第二季）

Private home rents in Singapore rose 0.7% in the second quarter of 2026, ending an extended stretch of softening that had put tenants in the stronger position at the negotiating table. The increase is modest, but it marks a clear shift in direction.

Rents had been flat or falling for several quarters as a wave of new completions added supply faster than the market could absorb it. That overhang is now being worked through, and with fewer new completions expected ahead, the balance is tilting back toward landlords.

For tenants whose leases are up for renewal soon, conditions are no longer as favourable as they were six months ago. If you are in a unit you are happy with, locking in a renewal before rents climb further could save you money. For landlords who accepted below-market rates to retain tenants through the soft patch, there is now a reasonable basis to bring rates back to market levels when leases expire.

新加坡私人住宅租金在2026年第二季上升0.7%，终结了一段持续较长、令租户在谈判桌上占据优势的疲软期。涨幅虽然温和，但方向已清晰转变。

此前连续数个季度租金持平或下跌，原因是大批新落成项目短期内增加了大量供应，市场一时难以消化。这一积压情况如今逐渐消退，加上预期往后新落成项目减少，供需天平正在重新向房东倾斜。

对于租约即将到期的租户来说，目前的条件已不如六个月前那般有利。如果你对现住的单位满意，趁租金进一步上涨前锁定续约，或许能为你省下一笔钱。对于在疲软期以低于市价的租金来留住租户的房东来说，当租约到期时，将租金调回市价水平，现在已有合理的依据。

What it means for you对你意味着什么
For landlords, rents are recovering; for tenants, costs are starting to creep back up after the recent correction, so locking in a longer lease now may save money later.对房东而言，租金正在回升；对租户而言，经历近期调整后租金成本开始重新攀升，现在锁定较长租约或许可以省下一笔开支。
