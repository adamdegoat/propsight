HDB resale market slows overall while million-dollar flat sales hit a recordHDB转售市场整体放缓，但百万元组屋成交量创历史新高

-0.3% HDB resale price change in Q2 20262026年第二季度HDB转售价格变动

HDB resale prices dipped 0.3% in the second quarter of 2026, the second consecutive quarterly decline. The cooling measures introduced over the past few years are clearly having an effect on the broader market, and sellers of typical flats are facing more resistance from buyers than they did a couple of years ago.

At the same time, the number of HDB flats sold above $1 million hit a new record high in the same period. The overall market is softening while the most coveted units in mature estates and popular blocks continue to attract buyers willing to pay top dollar. These are two very different markets operating under the same HDB banner.

What this means for you depends on what you are chasing. If you are looking at a regular resale flat, you are in a calmer environment where sellers need to be more competitive on price. If you have your eye on a specific block in a sought-after location, expect those sellers to hold firm because the demand supporting their asking price has not gone away.

2026年第二季度，HDB转售价格下滑0.3%，连续第二个季度录得跌幅。过去几年推出的降温措施显然已对整体市场产生效果，普通组屋的卖家正面临买家更强的议价压力，远不如几年前那般顺畅。

与此同时，同期售价超过$1 million的HDB组屋成交量却创下历史新高。整体市场在走软，而成熟市镇和热门楼栋中最抢手的单位，依然吸引着愿意出高价的买家。这两个市场同处HDB的旗帜下，却走着截然不同的轨迹。

这对你意味着什么，取决于你在追求什么。如果你看的是普通转售组屋，现在的环境相对宽松，卖家在定价上需要更有竞争力。如果你瞄准的是某个热门地段的特定楼栋，那里的卖家会守住价格，因为支撑他们开价的需求并没有消失。

What it means for you对你意味着什么
Most HDB buyers face a calmer, slightly cheaper market, but if you are chasing a mature-estate flat in a popular block, expect prices to stay stubbornly high.大多数HDB买家面对的是一个更平静、略微便宜一些的市场。但如果你瞄准的是成熟市镇热门楼栋的组屋，价格仍会顽强守在高位。
