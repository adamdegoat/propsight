Singapore Mortgage Rates Are Competitive Again, But The Cheapest Loan Is Not Always The Right One新加坡房贷利率重回吸引水平，但最低利率未必最适合你

Home loan rates in Singapore have fallen back to levels that make borrowing affordable again, and banks are competing actively for business. Buyers who held off because of high rates now have more reasonable options, and existing borrowers who locked in at peak rates have a genuine case to look at refinancing.

The headline rate, however, is only one part of the picture. A loan with a low advertised rate but a three-year lock-in and steep break penalties can cost more over time than a slightly higher rate with more flexibility, particularly if your situation might change before the lock-in ends. Legal fees, valuation costs and cashback promotions all affect the true cost of switching lenders.

The right move is to get full term sheets from at least three lenders and compare the effective cost across the entire lock-in period, not just the rate on the front page. If you are refinancing, calculate whether the savings over the lock-in window actually cover your switching costs before you commit. A broker can help with this, but make sure they are showing you options from banks they do not have exclusive fee arrangements with.

新加坡的房贷利率已回落到令借贷再度变得划算的水平，各银行也在积极争取业务。之前因利率高企而观望的买家，现在有了更合理的选择；在高峰期锁定贷款的现有借款人，也有充分理由考虑重新贷款。

不过，表面利率只是整体考量的一部分。广告利率低但锁定期三年、提前还款罚款高的贷款，从长远来看可能比略高利率但更灵活的方案更贵，尤其是当你的情况可能在锁定期结束前发生变化时。律师费、估价费以及现金返还优惠，都会影响转换银行的实际成本。

正确的做法是向至少三家银行索取完整的条款说明书，比较整个锁定期的实际成本，而不只是看第一页的利率。如果你在考虑重新贷款，先计算锁定期内能节省的金额是否足以覆盖转换成本，再做决定。按揭经纪可以协助你，但要确认他们推荐的选项不是只来自有独家收费安排的银行。

What it means for you对你意味着什么
If you are buying or refinancing, now is a good time to shop around, but compare the full package rather than just chasing the lowest advertised rate.无论是购房还是重新贷款，现在是货比三家的好时机，但要全面比较整体配套，而不只是追求最低的广告利率。
