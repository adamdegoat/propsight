Singapore mortgage rates are competitive again, but the cheapest loan is not always the best新加坡房贷利率重具竞争力，但最低利率未必是最好的选择

Home loan rates in Singapore have fallen back to levels that look genuinely competitive after the painful rate hikes of the past couple of years. Borrowers who have been waiting for rates to ease before buying or refinancing now have more to work with. But financial experts are warning that chasing the lowest advertised rate can backfire if you do not read the full terms.

The cheapest headline rates typically come with lock-in periods of one to three years, during which you cannot switch lenders without paying a penalty. If rates fall further after you lock in, or if you need to sell or refinance early for any reason, that penalty can easily wipe out all the savings the lower rate gave you.

The practical takeaway is to compare the complete package rather than just the interest rate. A loan with a modestly higher rate but no lock-in clause and lower fees can easily work out cheaper over a realistic holding period. Get quotes from several banks, ask about all the conditions attached, and read what you are signing before you commit.

经历过去几年令人痛苦的加息周期后，新加坡的房贷利率已回落至真正具竞争力的水平。一直在等利率回落再买房或再融资的借款人，现在有了更多选择空间。但金融专家提醒，一味追求最低挂牌利率，若不细读完整条款，反而可能得不偿失。

最低的挂牌利率通常附带一至三年的锁定期，期间若要转换贷款方，须缴交罚金。如果锁定后利率进一步下降，或者你因故需要提前出售或再融资，这笔罚金很可能将低利率带来的所有节省一笔勾销。

实际的做法，是比较完整的贷款配套，而不只是利率数字。一个利率稍高、但没有锁定期且费用较低的贷款，在实际持有期内往往更合算。向多家银行索取报价，问清楚所有附带条件，在签约前把你要签的东西看清楚。
