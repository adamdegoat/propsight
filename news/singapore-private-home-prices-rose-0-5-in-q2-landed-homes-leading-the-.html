Singapore Private Home Prices Rose 0.5% in Q2, Landed Homes Leading the Gains新加坡私宅价格第二季上涨0.5%，有地房产领涨

0.5% private home price rise q2私宅价格涨幅（第二季）

URA data shows private residential prices in Singapore rose 0.5% in the second quarter of 2026. Landed homes including terrace houses, semi-detached properties and bungalows drove most of the gain, while non-landed homes such as condominiums posted slower growth.

Landed properties have stayed firm because the supply of land zoned for that use is genuinely limited in Singapore. That scarcity keeps prices supported even when the broader private market cools. Condominiums face more competition from new launches and a larger pool of available units, which is why that segment is growing more slowly.

For buyers who are waiting for a sharp correction in private home prices before committing, this data does not support that bet. The market is moving gradually but it is still moving upward. A more practical approach is to focus on finding specific projects or locations where you see genuine value, rather than waiting for a broad price reset that the numbers suggest is unlikely.

URA数据显示，新加坡私人住宅价格在2026年第二季上涨0.5%。有地房产，包括排屋、半独立式房屋和独立洋房，是涨幅的主要来源，而condo等非有地住宅的升幅则较慢。

有地房产价格之所以保持稳固，是因为新加坡可供有地住宅发展的地块确实十分有限。这种稀缺性即使在私宅市场整体放缓时，仍能为价格提供支撑。Condo则面临更多来自新发售项目和市场现有单位的竞争，因此这个市场段的升幅更为缓慢。

对于等待私宅价格大幅回调才愿意出手的买家来说，这份数据并不支持这个策略。市场在缓慢但持续地上升。更务实的做法，是专注寻找你认为有真正价值的特定项目或地段，而不是等待一个数字显示不太可能发生的全面价格重置。
