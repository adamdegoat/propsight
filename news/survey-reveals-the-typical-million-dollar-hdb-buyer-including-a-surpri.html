Survey Reveals the Typical Million-Dollar HDB Buyer, Including a Surprising Trend调查揭示百万HDB买家的真实面貌，其中一个发现出乎意料

A new survey has put some numbers behind a question that comes up often: who is actually paying $1 million or more for an HDB resale flat? The findings push back against the assumption that these buyers are mostly investors or speculators chasing headlines.

The data suggests a large proportion of million-dollar HDB buyers are genuine owner-occupiers buying to live in, not to flip or rent out. That is a meaningful finding because it suggests the demand at the top of the HDB market is driven by real housing needs rather than thin speculative activity that could unwind quickly if sentiment shifts.

If you are trying to read where that segment of the market is heading, understanding who is actually buying and why gives you a more reliable foundation than just watching headline prices. A market supported by genuine owner-occupier demand is generally more stable than one driven by investor rotation, though prices can still soften if income growth stalls or interest rates move.

For sellers of large or well-located HDB flats, this is a useful signal that your pool of serious buyers may be broader and more committed than the sceptics suggest. For buyers wondering whether $1 million HDB prices are rational, knowing the demand is real rather than speculative is at least some reassurance.
