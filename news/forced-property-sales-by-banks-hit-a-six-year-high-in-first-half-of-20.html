Forced Property Sales by Banks Hit a Six-Year High in First Half of 20262026年上半年银行强制出售房产创六年新高

6-yr high mortgagee listings in H1 20262026年上半年强制出售挂牌数量

The number of homes being sold by banks to recover unpaid loans hit a six-year high in the first half of 2026, the highest level since 2020. These forced sales happen when a borrower defaults and the bank steps in to sell the property to get its money back.

The jump to a six-year high reflects the cumulative strain of several years of higher interest rates. Many buyers stretched their budgets during the run-up in property prices, and higher monthly repayments have pushed some of them to the point where they can no longer keep up. This is not a crisis-level number, but it is a meaningful signal that financial stress among some property owners is rising.

For buyers, distressed listings occasionally offer value, but they come with complications around timelines and conditions, so go in with your eyes open. For the broader market, a cluster of forced sales in a neighbourhood tends to drag on nearby valuations, at least in the short term. If you are planning to sell in an area where mortgagee sales have been appearing, do not anchor your expectations to peak prices from a year or two ago. Price based on what comparable units are actually transacting at today.

2026年上半年，银行为追讨未偿还贷款而强制出售房产的数量，创下六年新高，达到2020年以来的最高水平。这类强制出售发生在借款人违约、银行介入出售房产以收回资金之时。

创六年新高的背后，反映的是连续数年高利率环境下累积的财务压力。许多买家在房价上涨期间将预算拉伸到极限，更高的每月还款额已将部分人逼到无力维持的地步。这个数字还未到危机级别，但却是一个有意义的信号，说明部分房产业主的财务压力正在上升。

对于买家而言，被迫出售的房产偶尔能提供物超所值的机会，但在时间表和条件方面存在诸多复杂因素，入市前要保持清醒。对于大市而言，某个地区出现一批强制出售，往往会拖累附近的估值，至少在短期内如此。如果你计划在强制出售房产频繁出现的地区售房，不要以一两年前的高峰价格作为参考。应根据同类单位今天的实际成交价来定价。

What it means for you对你意味着什么
