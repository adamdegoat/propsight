$1.3M profit on privatised Tampines EC淡滨尼私有化EC的盈利

A privatised executive condominium in Tampines recently sold for $1.3 million more than the owner paid for it five years ago. That kind of gain over half a decade stands out even by Singapore's standards.

The privatisation milestone is the key driver. ECs start life as a hybrid product with restrictions on who can buy, but once the five-year mark passes, the flat opens up to all buyers including foreigners. That jump in eligible buyers typically lifts values sharply, and this transaction shows just how sharply it can move.

If you own an EC that has crossed the five-year privatisation line and have been sitting on the fence about selling, this deal is a useful reference point. The window to capture that uplift before newer supply arrives or market conditions shift does not stay open indefinitely.

淡滨尼一个已私有化的执行共管公寓（EC）最近易手，售价比屋主五年前购入时高出$1.3 million。以五年的时间跨度来说，这样的获利幅度即使放在新加坡楼市也相当亮眼。

私有化这个关键节点是最主要的价值推手。EC在初期是一种对买家资格有限制的混合产品，但过了五年期限后，就向所有买家开放，包括外国人。这意味着符合资格的买家群体大幅扩大，房价也随之显著上涨，这宗交易清楚说明了涨幅可以有多大。

如果你拥有一个已过五年私有化期的EC，一直在观望要不要出售，这笔交易是一个很好的参考。在新供应涌现或市场环境转变之前，把握这个升值机会的窗口不会一直等着你。
