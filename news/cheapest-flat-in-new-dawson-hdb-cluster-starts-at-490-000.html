That $490,000 figure is the entry point for the smallest flat type in the cluster. Resale 2-room flats in Queenstown trade across a wide range depending on floor, age, and remaining lease, so this launch price gives you a concrete number to hold up against what is already out there on the open market.

If you want a compact flat in a well-connected central area without paying full resale premiums, the Dawson launch is worth comparing directly against nearby resale listings. Just keep in mind that a new HDB flat comes with a wait to collect keys, while resale lets you move in on your own timeline.
