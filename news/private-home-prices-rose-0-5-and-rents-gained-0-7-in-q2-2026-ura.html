Private home prices rose 0.5% and rents gained 0.7% in Q2 2026: URAURA数据：2026年第二季度私宅价格上涨0.5%，租金涨0.7%

+0.5% Q2 2026 private home price rise2026年第二季度私宅价格涨幅

Official data from the Urban Redevelopment Authority shows private home prices rose 0.5% in the second quarter of 2026, with landed homes contributing most of the upward movement. Private residential rents also edged up, gaining 0.7% over the same period. Both figures are positive but measured, reflecting a market that has settled into a slower gear.

The pace is a far cry from the sharp quarterly gains that defined 2021 and 2022. What the numbers now show is a market that is still moving upward across both the buying and rental segments, but without the urgency or alarm that came with those earlier years. Sellers are still getting more than they paid, and landlords are still achieving rent increases, just not dramatic ones.

For buyers who have been holding out in hopes of a significant price correction, the data offers a gentle reality check. Prices are not falling in any meaningful way. If you have a real need to buy or move now, waiting for a correction that may not come could mean spending more on rent while you wait and still buying at similar prices when you eventually act.

URA（市区重建局）的官方数据显示，2026年第二季度私宅价格上涨0.5%，有地住宅贡献了大部分升幅。私宅租金同期也小幅上扬，录得0.7%的涨幅。两项数据均为正值，但幅度温和，反映市场已进入较平稳的运行节奏。

这与2021年和2022年每季度大幅飙升的情形已大相径庭。目前的数据呈现的是一个买卖和租赁两个板块均继续向上的市场，但已没有那几年令人忧虑的急速攀升。卖家仍在获利，房东仍能实现租金增长，只是幅度不再那么惊人。

对于一直在等候价格大幅回调的买家，这组数据提供了一个温和的现实提醒，价格并没有出现有意义的下跌。如果你有真实的购房或搬迁需求，等待一个可能不会来的调整，意味着你一边继续支付租金，一边等到最后还是以相近的价格买入。

What it means for you对你意味着什么
