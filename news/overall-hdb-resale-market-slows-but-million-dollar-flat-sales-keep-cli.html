Overall HDB Resale Market Slows, But Million-Dollar Flat Sales Keep ClimbingHDB转售市场整体放缓，但百万元组屋销量持续攀升

The overall HDB resale market has become quieter in 2026, with transaction volumes easing compared to the busier years before. But inside that slower market, one segment is moving in the opposite direction: flats priced above S$1 million are still selling in growing numbers.

The million-dollar HDB flat used to be rare news. It is now a regular fixture of the market, concentrated in large units in mature, well-connected estates. While the mass market has softened and buyers of mid-range flats have more time and more choice than before, the premium segment has not followed. The gap between the two ends is getting wider.

If you are selling a typical three-room or four-room flat outside a prime location, you need to price sharply to attract offers because buyers have more options than before. If you are buying or selling a large flat in a sought-after estate, do not expect any cooling at that end. The market has effectively split into two stories and which one applies to you depends entirely on what type and location of flat you are dealing with.

2026年整体HDB转售市场趋于平静，交易量与此前更热闹的年份相比有所回落。但在这个放缓的市场内部，有一个板块正朝着相反方向发展：售价超过S$1百万的组屋销量仍在持续增加。

百万元HDB组屋曾经是罕见新闻，如今已成为市场常态，集中于成熟、交通便利的市镇中的大型单位。大市虽已降温，中端组屋买家也拥有了更多时间和选择，但高端板块却没有跟随放缓。两个端口之间的差距正在扩大。

如果你正在出售位于非黄金地段的普通三房或四房组屋，必须精准定价才能吸引出价，因为买家的选择现在比以前多了。如果你是在买卖热门市镇的大单位，在那个板块别指望有任何降温。市场已经分化为两种走势，哪种走势适用于你，完全取决于你所涉及的单位类型和地点。
