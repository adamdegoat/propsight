Property agents must close more real deals to keep their licence from 20272027年起房产经纪须完成更多真实交易方可续牌

3 years new agent registration period新的经纪注册年限

From 2027, property agents in Singapore will need to demonstrate a minimum number of completed transactions before the Council for Estate Agencies will renew their licence. The change targets agents who hold a registration but rarely do any actual business.

At the same time, registration periods are being extended from one year to three years. Active agents benefit from less administrative renewal work, but the longer cycle also makes it harder for inactive agents to quietly roll over their licence year after year without scrutiny of what they have actually been doing.

For buyers and sellers, the practical result should be a market where the agent you deal with is more likely to have real, recent experience. An agent who has been trading regularly through the current market cycle is in a very different position from one who last closed a deal a few years ago and has been coasting on a stored credential since.

从2027年起，新加坡房产经纪在向产业代理理事会（CEA）续牌前，须证明自己完成了最低数量的交易宗数。这项改变针对的是那些持有注册资格却几乎不做实际生意的经纪人。

与此同时，注册期限也从一年延长至三年。活跃的经纪人因此减少了行政续牌的工作，但更长的注册周期同样意味着，不活跃的经纪人无法再年复一年悄悄续牌，而不受到对其实际从业情况的审查。

对买家和卖家来说，实际效果应该是市场上你所接触的经纪人，更有可能拥有真实、近期的从业经验。一个在当前市场周期中持续活跃成交的经纪人，和一个几年前最后一次完成交易、此后一直靠着一张存档资格混日子的人，完全是两码事。
