Condo resale volumes bounced back in June 20262026年6月condo转售成交量回弹

The condo resale market saw a noticeable pickup in activity in June 2026 after a quieter stretch. Transaction volumes bounced back up, suggesting that buyers who had been sitting on the fence decided the time was right to move. A rebound like this tends to reflect a mix of improving sentiment and a backlog of decisions finally being made.

This does not necessarily mean prices are jumping, but it does confirm that underlying demand for resale condos remains real. When buyers return to a market after holding back, it usually means they have decided that further waiting is not worth the risk of missing out or facing even less choice later.

For sellers, more activity means more potential buyers and a better chance of getting a deal done at a reasonable price. For buyers, the leverage you might have had during the quiet patch is starting to shrink. If you are in the market, being prepared and ready to act will matter more over the coming weeks than it did a few months ago.

这不一定意味着价格会急速上涨，但确实证明二手condo的潜在需求依然真实存在。当买家在观望一段时间后重新入市，通常意味着他们已判断：继续等下去，不值得冒错失机会或之后选择更少的风险。

对卖家而言，更多的市场活动意味着更多潜在买家，以合理价格成交的机会也更大。对买家而言，你在平静期可能握有的议价空间正在缩小。如果你正在看房，未来几周做好准备、随时可以出手，比几个月前更为重要。
